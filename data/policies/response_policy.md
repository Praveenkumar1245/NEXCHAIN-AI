# NexChain AI - Supply Chain Mitigation Policy Guidelines

## Priority Guidelines
1. **Tier 1 / High Priority Orders**: Must be fulfilled with maximum speed using cross-warehouse stock reallocation or air freight expediting.
2. **Shortage Resolution Order**:
   - Strategy 1: Reallocate stock from non-critical warehouse buffers (e.g. WH02 stock for WH01 orders).
   - Strategy 2: Expedite delayed inbound shipments via express logistics (e.g., convert SH101 to air freight).
   - Strategy 3: Partial fulfillment (ship available stock immediately, backorder remainder).
   - Strategy 4: Proactive customer notification & delivery date renegotiation.

## Risk Thresholds
- **Critical Risk**: Order promised date <= Disruption End Date AND Stock Shortage > 0.
- **Medium Risk**: Buffer days < 3 days.
- **Low Risk**: Adequate warehouse safety stock available to cover promised dates.
